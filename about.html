Our Journey

What started as a small group of animal lovers in 2010 has grown into a comprehensive animal welfare organization that has helped over 10,000 pets find their forever homes.

2010

PetPals was founded by Sarah Johnson in her backyard with just 5 rescued dogs.

2015

Opened our first shelter facility and reached 1,000 successful adoptions.

2023

Celebrated 10,000+ adoptions and expanded to three cities with 50+ volunteers.
